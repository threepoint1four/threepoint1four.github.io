Modeling the Future Challenge

Wheat-price data analysis and forecasting.

MTFC · Python · National finalist

For the Modeling the Future Challenge, our team studied wheat producer prices alongside production quantities and the value of imports and exports. I used Python to merge country-level data, compare these measures with producer prices using linear regression, and explore a seasonal price forecast for Egypt.

We were national finalists and scored 64 out of 72 points in the final round.
