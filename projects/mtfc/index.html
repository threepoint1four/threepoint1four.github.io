Modeling the Future Challenge

Wheat-price modeling with production, inflation, climate, and energy data.

Lead programmer · National finalist · 64/72 in the final round

I led the programming for our Modeling the Future Challenge entry, which reached the national finals.

Modeling work

In my main notebook, I worked with annual wheat data from 2000–2024. I checked the data for missing values and explored how average price related to production quantity, CPI, climate anomaly, oil price, and wheat use. Scatterplots and a correlation heatmap helped me inspect those relationships before modeling them.

I fit a multivariable linear regression to estimate average wheat price. I then projected several inputs from their historical trends, added oil-price assumptions, and passed them through the model. The notebook ends with exploratory price projections for 2025 and 2026.

Supporting analysis

I also wrote Python scripts that combined country-level wheat producer-price, production, import, and export data for comparison. A separate experiment explored seasonal price forecasting for Egypt.
