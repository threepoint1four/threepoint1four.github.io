Trips

A poker project with a hand evaluator, odds calculations, and a small Hold’em engine.

Python · Poker · In progress

Trips started with a hand evaluator and grew into an odds calculator and a small terminal Hold’em engine. It is still in progress.

One hand

Say I have A♥ K♥ and the flop is Q♥ 7♣ 2♥. I have four hearts, with two cards still to come. Trips can fill in the turn, river, and one opponent’s hand, then compare our best five-card hands.

from hand_evaluator.cards import Card
from odds_evaluator.monte_carlo import estimate_odds

hole = [Card.from_string(card) for card in ("AH", "KH")]
flop = [Card.from_string(card) for card in ("QH", "7C", "2H")]
equity = estimate_odds(hole, flop, runs=100_000)

The estimate varies between runs. A win counts as one, a tie as half, and a loss as zero.

Inside Trips

  • Hand evaluator. It ranks five-card hands and uses kickers to break ties. Given seven cards, it checks every five-card combination and keeps the best.
  • Odds. Monte Carlo sampling estimates equity against one random opponent. The exact calculator enumerates the remaining board and opponent cards; it is more practical once fewer cards remain unknown.
  • Hold’em engine. A terminal prototype deals cards and moves through community cards and basic betting rounds. I am still working through its game-flow edge cases.

Starting hands

I also ran preflop simulations for every ordered pair of distinct cards and averaged them into the 169 familiar starting-hand categories. The chart shows estimated equity against one random opponent. Each pair used 100,000 sampled runouts; ties count as half a win.
