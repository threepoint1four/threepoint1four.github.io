Trips

A poker project with a hand evaluator, odds calculations, and a small Hold’em engine.

Python · Poker · In progress

Trips ranks and compares poker hands, estimates win odds through Monte Carlo simulations, and can enumerate possible outcomes for exact odds. Its terminal-based Hold’em engine runs a basic game flow with players, community cards, and betting rounds.
