This is a place to collect things I find interesting: math problems, competitive programming ideas, research experience, machine learning, and the occasional tangent. Some entries will be careful explanations. Others might just be a question I want to come back to.

The organization is deliberately loose. Notes are for thinking and writing; projects are for things that have taken a more concrete shape. A poker idea, for example, could begin as a note and later become a project. There is no need to decide that in advance.
